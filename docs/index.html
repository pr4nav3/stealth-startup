A focus timer that speaks up when you're distracted.

Supervisor watches the window you're in while a session runs. Stay on a distraction too long and it says something out loud, in a voice you cloned yourself. Your mom, your manager, maybe even you. Whoever you'd least like to be caught by. It all runs on your own machine.

Features

A short list, on purpose.

Supervisor only runs while a session is on. Start one and it stays with you until you stop it.

01

Focus sessions

Set a goal, a length, and how strict it should be.

02

Spoken nudges

Stay on a distraction long enough and it speaks up in the voice you picked.

03

Your distractions

Five common ones are on the list. Add yours. Everything else counts as work.

04

Local history

Finished sessions are saved on your machine. Read them or delete them.

Pause it, snooze it, or correct it mid session

Supervisor sits in the tray with a small window you can pin over everything else. Snooze the nudges for a few minutes, or tell it that what it just flagged is the work today.

Voice

A reminder is easier to ignore when it's a notification

Record about twenty five seconds of someone talking and Supervisor builds a voice from it. Keep as many as you like and switch between them per session.

Every line it says is text you can rewrite. Clips are made in advance, so a nudge plays the second you need it. Nothing is uploaded.

Camera · optional

Closing the tab doesn't help if the phone is in your hand

Switch the camera on and Supervisor can tell whether you're at your desk, away from it, or looking at your phone. It only counts phone use when it can actually see the phone, so glancing down at your desk won't be held against you.

The camera stays off until you turn it on, and the app works fine if you never do. Frames are read and thrown away.

Privacy

There's no server behind this.

No account, no sign in, nothing phoning home.

What it reads

  • The app in frontWhich program you're looking at.
  • The window title What tells a work tab from a distraction.
  • Idle timeSo it stays quiet when you've walked away.
  • Camera labels, if you turn the camera on Never the picture.

What it never touches

  • What you type
  • Your screenNo screenshots, no scanning.
  • Page contents or browsing history
  • Anything outside your machine Recordings, clips and camera frames stay put.
  • Accounts, telemetry, analytics, ads

All of it lives in one folder on your computer. Delete a voice, a session, or the whole history whenever you want.
